Supplier handbook. This handbook explains how we work with our suppliers, from the first order to the last invoice. Read it once in full, then keep it as a reference.

If anything about opening an account with us is unclear, ask your contact in purchasing before you act, not after. We review the rules on opening an account with us every year, and we tell you in writing at least a month before any change takes effect. Where this section and a signed agreement disagree about opening an account with us, the signed agreement wins.
